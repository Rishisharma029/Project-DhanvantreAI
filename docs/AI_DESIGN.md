## 2. Guardrails against Hallucinations

- **Never allow unsupported statements**: Every medical claim generated by the system must map to an authoritative evidence chunk (WHO, CDC, NICE, ICMR, FDA).
- Statements failing evidence cross-verification are automatically sanitized into `[REDACTED_UNVERIFIED_CLAIM]`.
