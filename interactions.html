DANGEROUS — Major Contraindication Alert
Score: 9.2 / 10

Pharmacological Mechanism

Co-administration of Warfarin (Anticoagulant) and Aspirin (Antiplatelet) causes synergistically elevated risk of major gastrointestinal hemorrhage and intracranial bleeding. Aspirin inhibits COX-1 platelet aggregation while Warfarin depletes Vitamin K clotting factors.

Recommended Clinical Action

Avoid concurrent non-indicated therapy. If dual antiplatelet/anticoagulant therapy is medically necessary, co-prescribe a Proton Pump Inhibitor (e.g. Omeprazole) and monitor INR closely (target 2.0 - 2.5).
